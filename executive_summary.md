# Executive Summary — UAC Care Transition Efficiency

**For:** HHS / ORR program and policy stakeholders
**Data:** 720 daily HHS UAC reports, January 12, 2023 – December 21, 2025

## What we measured
How quickly children move through the pipeline (CBP custody → HHS care → sponsor placement), not just how many are in custody.

## Key findings
1. **Discharge throughput is the binding constraint.** HHS care peaked at 11,516 children on December 20, 2023. It fell 78% to 2,484 by December 2025, mostly because intake dropped from roughly 130 per day to about 8 per day.
2. **Placement effectiveness fell significantly after mid-2024.** The median daily discharge rate, as a share of HHS population, fell from 3.15% to 0.86% (Mann-Whitney p < 0.0001).
3. **A severe disruption occurred from February 17 to March 31, 2025.** Discharges fell to about 24% of their prior 30-day level. Within that window, a stagnation run covered 14 reports across 20 calendar days.
4. **The current discharge rate cannot clear the backlog quickly.** At about 9 discharges per day, clearing 2,484 children in 90 days would need 27.6 per day (+208%). A 30-day clearance would need 82.8 per day (+823%).
5. **Placements became less predictable in 2025.** The Outcome Stability Score (0–100, higher means steadier discharges) averaged 77 in 2023 and 2024 but fell to 54.9 in 2025, with a low of 6.5 right after the March 2025 stall. The backlog shrank by about 18 children per report on average in 2024–2025, but it grew again on the latest report (+6.4).
6. **The weekday/weekend gap in reports comes from the reporting schedule, not from operations.** Reports are filed Monday–Thursday and Sunday, almost never Friday or Saturday.

## Recommendations
- Investigate the cause of the February–March 2025 disruption (policy, staffing, sponsor vetting, or reporting).
- Adopt a live alert when the daily discharge rate stays below 0.5% of HHS population for 5 or more reports.
- Set a discharge-rate floor tied to population, and publish the reporting window behind each figure.
- Obtain case-level length-of-stay data. It is the direct measure of time to reunification.
